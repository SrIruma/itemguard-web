Seals & reclaim

An item with pickupable=false that lands on the ground would lie there forever: nobody can pick it up, not even its owner. A seal turns that limbo into a place.

Bedrock under the item · label above it · the item itself is never touched

What a seal is

When a guarded item ends up on the ground, the plugin lays a block underneath it and places a floating label above it:

  • Bedrock where the ground can take one — air, grass, a plant or a snow layer. On bare stone the label appears on its own.
  • A label naming the item, its owner and the command that takes it out.

The seal never contains the item: the item itself is untouched, /ig find still locates it, and a lost seal costs nothing but the marker.

Hoppers, fire, explosions, despawn and liquids are all refused around a seal, and a periodic pass repairs any marker that is damaged or missing.

Taking it out

For the owner: /ig reclaim

/ig reclaim                # looks for the nearest seal, no id needed
/ig reclaim TI-1a2b3c      # or name the item

The owner gets three attempts per window (24 hours by default), and needs no id — it finds the nearest seal, so an item that was paid for does not have to be looked up first.

The budget is not refunded by a success. A successful reclaim does not hand an attempt back: otherwise a player could drop and reclaim the same item forever and the limit would never bind. Only the window closing clears it. Once the attempts are spent, the seal stays put and an admin takes the item out with /ig recover.

For an admin: /ig recover and /ig unseal

/ig recover TI-1a2b3c      # take the item out, in one piece, wherever it is
/ig unseal TI-1a2b3c       # take the seal off without moving the item

The seal block in config.yml

seal:
  enabled: true            # master switch for everything below
  bedrock: true            # lay bedrock under the item where the ground allows it
  hologram: true           # place the label above it
  sweep-seconds: 10        # how often to look for items to seal and seals to repair
  reclaim:
    max-attempts: 3        # attempts the owner gets per window
    window-seconds: 86400  # how long that window lasts
    radius: 8.0            # how far /ig reclaim looks when given no id

Every number is clamped into a range that cannot wedge the server: a negative budget or a zero-length window would either lock every owner out forever or hand out unlimited attempts.

Sweep and unloaded chunks Only loaded chunks are checked by the sweep pass, so an item in an unloaded chunk is sealed the moment someone loads it. Set sweep-seconds: 0 to turn the pass off — items already sealed stay sealed but will not be repaired. A bedrock left behind by a seal taken down while its chunk was away is queued and removed when the chunk comes back, so the next seal on that spot can always get a bedrock of its own.

Permissions around seals

itemguard.reclaim is the one permission granted to everyone by default: owning an item is enough to get it back. Revoke it on a group and its players are sent to an admin instead. itemguard.seal controls both /ig seal and /ig unseal. See Permissions.
