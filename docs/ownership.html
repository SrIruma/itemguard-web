Ownership & transfer

The owner is not a name in a record. The owner is who the protections answer to — and that is why moving an item and moving its ownership are two different things that you do together.

Read on: /ig give · /ig transfer · /ig owner

What ownership means

The recorded owner is the only player who:

  • may break the block the item was placed as;
  • is rescued for when an explosion hits the container holding it;
  • is queued for when a recovered item arrives on death;
  • may reclaim a sealed item on the ground.

Transferring an item without transferring ownership would split those: the new holder could walk around with the item while explosions still sent it back to the old owner.

Handing an item to a player

/ig give Steve Sword amount 64      # build and deliver, Steve becomes the owner
/ig transfer Steve TI-1a2b3c         # send an existing tracked item on
/ig transfer Steve hand              # ...or let it come from your hand

Transferring ownership: the confirmation

/ig owner <player> [id|hand] moves both the item and its ownership — but it is confirmed by the current owner, not just by the admin running the command:

/ig owner Alex TI-1a2b3c       -> Alex is asked to accept
/ig owner confirm              -> run by the current owner

Two rules follow from that:

  • Both players must be online. That is the point: nobody can quietly hand an item away from an account that has been hijacked, and nothing gets queued for a recipient who is not there.
  • A request does not survive a restart. An owner coming back days later will not find a stale transfer waiting.

raw — change ownership without moving the item

/ig owner Alex TI-1a2b3c raw

For a sale where a middleman passes the item on without ever holding it: the record changes hands, the item stays where it is.

History

The history belongs to the object, not to whoever held it. A transfer is recorded as TRANSFER with both names, and the old entries are left alone. /ig info <id> opens the item's record on screen, and its history button shows the full event list.
